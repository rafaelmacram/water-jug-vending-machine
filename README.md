# Automated Water-Jug Vending Machine

Redesign of a cabinet-type vending machine for 20-litre water jugs, with an automatic wash and fill cycle and a capacity of **3 jugs at the same time**.

## Problem
In Mexico, filling water jugs in cabinet-type vending machines takes long because each machine handles one jug and the wash and fill cycles need manual steps. The redesign automates both cycles and works with up to 3 jugs at once, which triples the capacity of a conventional machine.

## What the project covers
- **Mechanical:** SolidWorks design of the cabinet, rotating structure, pneumatic gripper, jug-flipping mechanism and drainage; rotational inertia, torque and pump-power calculations (Bernoulli); selection of the compressor, pumps, valves, motors and gearmotor.
- **Electrical:** protections, conductors and conduit sized according to NOM-001-SEDE-2012 (Art. 430); power and single-line diagrams.
- **Control:** Siemens S7-1200 PLC with ladder logic (timers, counters and internal memories), sensors, a frequency drive for the main motor and a TP700 Comfort HMI.
